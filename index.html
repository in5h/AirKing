About Us

Who we are

AirKing (艾金空气工程) is an air engineering company specialising in industrial air conditioning, humidification and dust filtration systems. From the first site survey to commissioning and after-sales service, we deliver complete air solutions that protect product quality and reduce energy use.

In Pakistan, AirKing is represented from Faisalabad — the heart of the country's textile industry — so customers get local engineering support, fast spare-parts supply and on-site service.

  • Complete turnkey air engineering projects
  • In-house design, manufacturing and installation
  • Energy-efficient systems with intelligent controls
  • Local sales, service and spare-parts support

What We Make

Products & solutions

Proven equipment engineered for demanding, round-the-clock production environments.

Air Washer & Humidification Plants

Central air conditioning plants that control temperature and humidity precisely for spinning, weaving and processing halls.

Axial Flow Fans

High-efficiency fans with optimised blade profiles for large air volumes at low energy consumption.

Rotary Drum Filters

Continuous self-cleaning filtration that separates fibre and dust from return air.

Dust & Waste Collection Systems

Waste removal and compacting systems that keep machines and working areas clean.

High-Pressure Humidification

Fine-mist nozzle systems for direct room humidification and evaporative cooling.

Intelligent Control Systems

PLC and VFD-based automation that adjusts the plant to conditions in real time to save energy.
