Company Profile

Who we are

Shanghai Air King Air Equipment Co., Ltd. was founded in 1996. Our factory is located in Taicang City / Jinjiang City, Jiangsu Province, China, and covers an area of 25,000 square meters with more than 150 professional staff.

For three decades we have been deeply engaged in the textile air conditioning market, and our energy-saving automatic control systems rank among the most advanced in the industry. We are a highly competitive textile AC enterprise both domestically and internationally.

  • 30% market share in China's mid-to-high-end textile AC
  • Annual output value of textile AC products exceeds US$ 45 million
  • Products exported to over 10 countries
  • Local sales & service support in Faisalabad, Pakistan

Our journey since 1996

Milestones that shaped AirKing into a leading textile air conditioning brand.

  1. 1996

    Founded in Shanghai

    AirKing established the OEM factory for AC systems in Shanghai.

  2. 2003

    First automation system

Premium Equipment of AirKing

Equipment

Every component of an AirKing plant is chosen or designed for narrow tolerances, long life and low energy use.

01 Automation & Controls

Electric Panels

Rittal-equivalent quality enclosures — Rittal GmbH & Co. KG (Germany) is the world's largest maker of industrial electrical enclosures.

Inverters & Automation System

ABB inverters with original BECKHOFF (Germany) automation technology.

Electrical Components

Switchgear and components from ABB or Schneider for dependable, long-life operation.

Temperature & Humidity Sensors

BECKHOFF (Germany) sensors feeding precise, real-time values to the control system.

02 Air Washer

Showering Area

Air washer showering chamber built by AirKing for efficient humidification and cooling.

Water Spray Nozzles

Nozzles designed by AirKing for an even, efficient spray pattern.

Eliminator Plates

Separate water droplets from the conditioned air before it enters the production hall.

03 Air Handling

Supply & Return Air Fan

Energy-saving axial fans with aerodynamic blades, developed by AirKing for large air volumes.

Return Air Fan

Draws air back from the production hall to the plant for filtration and reconditioning.

Air Control Dampers

Dampers with auto drive motors, positioned by the control system to mix fresh and return air.

Weather Control Louvre

Fresh-air intake louvres that keep out rain and debris.

04 Filtration & Dust Handling

Rotary Air Filtration System

LDF rotary air filter — continuous filtration of fibre and dust from the return air.

Dust Collection System

Centralized collection, filtration and bailing of waste and dust.

Where We Work

Industries we serve

We provide complete air solutions for the following industries.

Spinning

Ring spinning, air-jet spinning, compact spinning and vortex spinning.

Weaving

Dual-dewpoint air supply for large and small weaving halls — yarn-dyed and raw white fabric.

Synthetic Fibre

Constant airflow and cooling capacity for each process section to hold temperature steady.

Knitted Fabric

Air conditioning, refrigeration and direct-air-delivery systems for warp and weft knitting.

Non-woven Fabric

Air conditioning systems for non-woven fabric workshops across their various processes.

Other Industries

Medical dressings (preheating and analytical rooms), papermaking, fibre glass, tyre and cigarette factories.
